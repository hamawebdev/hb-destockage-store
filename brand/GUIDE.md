# Comptoir de Luxe — brand guide

Version 1.1 · November 2026 · Printable version: [`guide.html`](guide.html) (one self-contained file:
open it in a browser and print, or save as PDF).

Comptoir de Luxe sells good products at clearance prices to customers all over Algeria. Customers order
online, we call them to confirm, and they pay cash when the parcel arrives. The brand has to make that
simple promise feel **trustworthy and calm**: a clean, spacious, modern shop where the price, the fee
and the total are always clear.

**The name.** One name, written two ways, never mixed or abbreviated in text:

| | Written exactly | Where |
|---|---|---|
| French / Latin | **Comptoir de Luxe** | French pages, Latin-script contexts (courier labels, sheet titles, e-mail), the logo |
| Arabic | كونتوار دو لوكس | Arabic pages and every Arabic sentence |
| Domain | **comptoirdeluxe.com** | `/ar/` (default) and `/fr/`; admin: `admin.comptoirdeluxe.com` |

Capital C and L, lower-case *de*; capitals only inside the logo. Never "CDL", "Comptoir", "Comptoir De
Luxe" or another Arabic spelling. «CL» exists only as the monogram. Full writing rules:
[`copy/VOICE.md`](copy/VOICE.md).

**Reference.** The look follows apple.com: light surfaces (#ffffff and #f5f5f7), near-black text,
one blue accent, generous space, crisp sans-serif type, photos of products on light backgrounds.
**Where the reference and the shop's speed and accessibility budgets disagree, the budgets win**: no
Latin webfont (the phone's own font is used), one Arabic font file in one weight, contrast ≥ 4.5:1,
motion in CSS only.

## 1. Personality

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Clear: price, fee, total, next step | Clever, cryptic, jargon |
| Calm and sure | Loud: "!!!", countdowns, flashing badges |
| Warm and respectful | Familiar or pushy |
| Modern and premium | Cheap-looking, cluttered, "souk" banners |
| Algerian, bilingual (Arabic first) | A translated foreign shop |

## 2. Logo

Files and full rules: [`logo/USAGE.md`](logo/USAGE.md). All marks are **outlined paths**: they need no font.

- **Wordmark** `COMPTOIR · DE LUXE`: the bold first word, a blue dot, light tracked capitals. Default on
  white or #f5f5f7; inverse on #1d1d1f; mono (one colour) for print.
- **Monogram** `CL` on a dark rounded tile with the blue dot in the corner: favicon, app icon, social avatars.
- **The blue dot** is a price-tag eyelet. It is the only colour in the logo and the same blue as the
  order button.
- **Clear space:** at least ½ of the cap height (x) around the wordmark; 1 x when possible. 25 % of
  the tile around the monogram.
- **Minimum size:** wordmark cap height 10 px, about 135 px wide (header: 14 px cap = 189 px wide on phones,
  18 px = 243 px from 768 px); monogram 16 px.
- **Never** retype the logo in a font, stretch it, recolour it, add shadows, put the tile in another box,
  or mirror it on Arabic pages. The Latin wordmark is the logo in both languages; in Arabic text the
  name is written كونتوار دو لوكس.
- No Arabic logo. Where the Arabic name must sit with the logo (shop sign, packaging), it is live text
  under the wordmark in Noto Sans Arabic 400, `text-secondary`, never part of the drawn mark.

## 4. Typography

| | French and Latin text | Arabic text |
|---|---|---|
| Font | The phone's system font: SF Pro (iPhone), Roboto (Android), Segoe UI (Windows) | **Noto Sans Arabic**, one weight (400), self-hosted, ~12 KB |
| Why | 0 bytes to download: the page appears instantly on any network | The only small Arabic font that covers ڨ ڢ ڧ; one weight keeps the page fast |
| Weights | 400 body, 600 headings, prices, buttons | 400 only. Hierarchy comes from **size, colour and space**, never bold |
| Line height | 1.5 body, 1.15 headings, 1.08 display | 1.7 body, 1.6 headings and UI |
| Letter spacing | −0.015 em on display/h1, −0.01 em on h2–h3 | **Never.** Spacing breaks the joins between Arabic letters |

**Type scale** (fluid between a 360 px phone and a 1440 px desktop; same steps in both languages):

| Step | Role | Phone 360 | Desktop 1440 |
|---|---|---|---|
| −2 | caption | 13 px | 13 px |
| −1 | small, hints | 14 | 15 |
| 0 | body | 16 | 17 |
| 1 | lead, h4, price | 19 | 21 |
| 2 | h3, card title | 23 | 27 |
| 3 | h2, section | 28 | 33 |
| 4 | h1, page title | 33 | 42 |
| 5 | display | 40 | 52 |
| 6 | hero (home only) | 48 | 64 |

Ratio 1.2 on phones, 1.25 on desktop. Running text is at most 68 characters wide. Prices and phone
numbers use tabular figures. Western digits (0–9) in both languages, as in Algeria. Uppercase eyebrows
exist in French only (Arabic has no capitals: use `text-secondary` instead).

## 5. Iconography

21 outline icons in `packages/ui-tokens/icons/` (owned by the tokens package): search, menu, close, bag,
chevron-start, chevron-end, arrow-start, arrow-end, phone, whatsapp, truck, check, plus, minus, info,
alert, filter, home, grid, user, star.

- **Grid and stroke:** 24 × 24 grid, 2 px padding, 1.5 px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills. Thin
  and crisp, like the reference.
- **Sizes:** 16 px (inline in captions), 20 px (next to UI text), 24 px (header, buttons). Nothing else.
- **Colour:** the colour of the text next to it. Status icons take the status colour (alert + danger,
  check + success) and always sit next to words.
- **Right-to-left:** only the four directional icons (chevron-start/-end, arrow-start/-end) flip on
  Arabic pages. "start" means back/previous, "end" means next/forward, in both languages. The truck,
  phone, search, WhatsApp and every other icon never flip.
- **Accessibility:** an icon-only button has an accessible name in the page language and a 44 × 44 px
  hit area.
- **WhatsApp** is a simple outline glyph on a plain `wa.me` link — never a chat widget, never the green logo.

## 6. Photography

Products are the heroes; the photos must look like one family.

- **Background:** seamless white or light grey (#f5f5f7). No coloured backdrops, no busy rooms on the
  main photo.
- **Framing:** square (1:1). The product is centred and fills about 80 % of the frame, with the same
  margin on every photo. Same camera height for the same kind of product.
- **Light:** soft, even daylight-like light; a small natural shadow under the product. No harsh flash,
  no strong colour cast.
- **Sequence per product:** 1) the product alone, front three-quarter view; 2) a second angle or the
  back; 3) details (material, size, label); 4) optionally one in-use photo. **Packs:** first photo
  shows every item of the pack arranged together, then one photo per item.
- **Variants:** one photo per colour, same framing, so switching colour doesn't make the page jump.
- **Never:** text, prices, logos or watermarks on the photo; stretched or low-resolution images;
  photos taken from other websites.
- **Today's photos are a temporary exception.** Some current product photos (from the client's
  existing site and suppliers) still carry burned-in prices or supplier renders whose rights are
  being confirmed (see `data/photos/README.md`). They are flagged there and must be replaced by
  photos that follow these rules before launch.
- **Files:** originals at least 1600 px on the long side, JPEG or PNG. The build creates AVIF at
  360/480/720/960/1200 px automatically. Placeholder photos are marked `placeholder: true`.

## 7. Layout and spacing

- **Phone first:** designed at 360 px, checked at 768 and 1440 px. Desktop is still 44 % of visitors.
- **Spacing** on a 4 / 8 px rhythm: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128.
- **Page gutter:** 16 px (phone) → 40 px (desktop). **Section spacing:** 48 → 96 px. Content up to
  1200 px wide; text pages up to 720 px.
- **Product grid:** 2 columns (phone), 3 (768), 4 (1024+); 12 → 24 px gaps.
- **Corners:** 8 (badges), 12 (inputs, notes), 18 (cards, image wells), 28 (home hero tiles); buttons
  are pills.
- **Depth** comes from white vs light-grey surfaces, not shadows. A soft shadow appears only on card
  hover, under the sticky header once scrolled, and on dialogs.
- **Direction:** every layout is written with start/end (logical) properties, so the Arabic page is a
  true mirror of the French one. Prices, phone numbers and order references stay left-to-right inside
  Arabic text.
- **Touch:** every tappable thing is at least 44 × 44 px. The sticky header (52 px) and the mobile
  order bar (64 px) never hide the field you are typing in.

## 8. Motion

Quiet and quick, CSS only (no animation libraries).

- Durations: 100 ms (press), 160 ms (hover, focus), 240 ms (dialogs, page transitions), 400 ms max.
- Easing: standard ease for most things; a slightly emphasised curve for things entering.
- Page-to-page: a soft cross-fade (View Transitions). Elements move at most 8 px.
- Nothing moves on its own: no auto-advancing carousels, no looping animations.
- With "reduce motion" switched on in the phone settings, all motion is turned off.

## 9. Voice

Full rules and examples: [`copy/VOICE.md`](copy/VOICE.md). In short: clear before clever, facts not
adjectives, calm (no "!"), warm and respectful.

| Arabic — do | Arabic — don't |
|---|---|
| اطلب الآن، وادفع عند الاستلام. | !!! اطلب بسرعة قبل نفاد الكمية |
| بقيت قطعتان فقط | آخر فرصة!!! |
| نتصل بك لتأكيد الطلب قبل الإرسال. | سيتم التواصل معكم في أقرب الآجال |

| French — do | French — don't |
|---|---|
| Payez à la réception. Livraison dans les 69 wilayas. | PAIEMENT À LA LIVRAISON !!! |
| Ce numéro n'est pas valide. Saisissez un mobile algérien à 10 chiffres. | Numéro incorrect !! |
| Plus que 3 en stock | Dépêchez-vous, il n'en reste presque plus ! |

Arabic is Modern Standard Arabic with the commerce words Algerians use (الدفع عند الاستلام، الولاية،
البلدية، سعر التوصيل) and Algerian month names (جانفي، فيفري…). French uses *vous*. All interface text
lives in `copy/strings.ar.json` and `copy/strings.fr.json`; page texts in `data/content/`.

## 10. Accessibility checklist

Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (text) and ≥ 3:1 (controls, focus) · visible focus ring (2 px blue, 2 px offset) ·
44 px tap targets · errors in words next to the field, never colour alone · language marked on every
mixed-language span · phone numbers in left-to-right isolation · reduced motion respected · no
CAPTCHA puzzle · works without JavaScript.
