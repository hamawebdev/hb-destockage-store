Brand guide · v1.0 · November 2026

HB Destockage sells good products at clearance prices to customers all over Algeria. Customers order online, we call them to confirm, and they pay cash when the parcel arrives. The brand has to make that simple promise feel trustworthy and calm: a clean, spacious, modern shop where the price, the fee and the total are always clear.

Reference. The look follows apple.com: light surfaces (#ffffff and #f5f5f7), near-black text, one blue accent, generous space, crisp sans-serif type, photos of products on light backgrounds. Where the reference and the shop's speed and accessibility budgets disagree, the budgets win: no Latin webfont (the phone's own font is used), one Arabic font file in one weight, contrast ≥ 4.5:1, motion in CSS only.

2. Logo

Files and full rules: logo/USAGE.md (logo/USAGE.md). All marks are outlined paths: they need no font.

  • Wordmark HB · DESTOCKAGE: bold initials, a blue dot, light tracked capitals. Default on white or #f5f5f7; inverse on #1d1d1f; mono (one colour) for print.
  • Monogram HB on a dark rounded tile with the blue dot in the corner: favicon, app icon, social avatars.
  • The blue dot is a price-tag eyelet. It is the only colour in the logo and the same blue as the order button.
  • Clear space: at least ½ of the cap height (x) around the wordmark; 1 x when possible. 25 % of the tile around the monogram.
  • Minimum size: wordmark cap height 10 px (header: 14 px on phones, 18 px from 768 px); monogram 16 px.
  • Never retype the logo in a font, stretch it, recolour it, add shadows, put the tile in another box, or mirror it on Arabic pages. The Latin name is the brand name in both languages; inside Arabic text it is written HB Destockage in <bdi lang="fr">.
  • No Arabic lockup: a transliteration reads awkwardly. If a descriptor is ever needed, it is live text under the wordmark, not part of the logo.

5. Iconography

Same four directional icons on an Arabic page (they flip; nothing else does):

21 outline icons in packages/ui-tokens/icons/ (owned by the tokens package): search, menu, close, bag, chevron-start, chevron-end, arrow-start, arrow-end, phone, whatsapp, truck, check, plus, minus, info, alert, filter, home, grid, user, star.

  • Grid and stroke: 24 × 24 grid, 2 px padding, 1.5 px stroke, round caps and joins, no fills. Thin and crisp, like the reference.
  • Sizes: 16 px (inline in captions), 20 px (next to UI text), 24 px (header, buttons). Nothing else.
  • Colour: the colour of the text next to it. Status icons take the status colour (alert + danger, check + success) and always sit next to words.
  • Right-to-left: only the four directional icons (chevron-start/-end, arrow-start/-end) flip on Arabic pages. "start" means back/previous, "end" means next/forward, in both languages. The truck, phone, search, WhatsApp and every other icon never flip.
  • Accessibility: an icon-only button has an accessible name in the page language and a 44 × 44 px hit area.
  • WhatsApp is a simple outline glyph on a plain wa.me link — never a chat widget, never the green logo.

6. Photography

Products are the heroes; the photos must look like one family.

  • Background: seamless white or light grey (#f5f5f7). No coloured backdrops, no busy rooms on the main photo.
  • Framing: square (1:1). The product is centred and fills about 80 % of the frame, with the same margin on every photo. Same camera height for the same kind of product.
  • Light: soft, even daylight-like light; a small natural shadow under the product. No harsh flash, no strong colour cast.
  • Sequence per product: 1) the product alone, front three-quarter view; 2) a second angle or the back; 3) details (material, size, label); 4) optionally one in-use photo. Packs: first photo shows every item of the pack arranged together, then one photo per item.
  • Variants: one photo per colour, same framing, so switching colour doesn't make the page jump.
  • Never: text, prices, logos or watermarks on the photo; stretched or low-resolution images; photos taken from other websites.
  • Today's photos are a temporary exception. Some current product photos (from the client's existing site and suppliers) still carry burned-in prices or supplier renders whose rights are being confirmed (see data/photos/README.md). They are flagged there and must be replaced by photos that follow these rules before launch.
  • Files: originals at least 1600 px on the long side, JPEG or PNG. The build creates AVIF at 360/480/720/960/1200 px automatically. Placeholder photos are marked placeholder: true.

7. Layout and spacing

  • Phone first: designed at 360 px, checked at 768 and 1440 px. Desktop is still 44 % of visitors.
  • Spacing on a 4 / 8 px rhythm: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128.
  • Page gutter: 16 px (phone) → 40 px (desktop). Section spacing: 48 → 96 px. Content up to 1200 px wide; text pages up to 720 px.
  • Product grid: 2 columns (phone), 3 (768), 4 (1024+); 12 → 24 px gaps.
  • Corners: 8 (badges), 12 (inputs, notes), 18 (cards, image wells), 28 (home hero tiles); buttons are pills.
  • Depth comes from white vs light-grey surfaces, not shadows. A soft shadow appears only on card hover, under the sticky header once scrolled, and on dialogs.
  • Direction: every layout is written with start/end (logical) properties, so the Arabic page is a true mirror of the French one. Prices, phone numbers and order references stay left-to-right inside Arabic text.
  • Touch: every tappable thing is at least 44 × 44 px. The sticky header (52 px) and the mobile order bar (64 px) never hide the field you are typing in.

8. Motion

Quiet and quick, CSS only (no animation libraries).

  • Durations: 100 ms (press), 160 ms (hover, focus), 240 ms (dialogs, page transitions), 400 ms max.
  • Easing: standard ease for most things; a slightly emphasised curve for things entering.
  • Page-to-page: a soft cross-fade (View Transitions). Elements move at most 8 px.
  • Nothing moves on its own: no auto-advancing carousels, no looping animations.
  • With "reduce motion" switched on in the phone settings, all motion is turned off.

10. Accessibility checklist

Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (text) and ≥ 3:1 (controls, focus) · visible focus ring (2 px blue, 2 px offset) · 44 px tap targets · errors in words next to the field, never colour alone · language marked on every mixed-language span · phone numbers in left-to-right isolation · reduced motion respected · no CAPTCHA puzzle · works without JavaScript.
